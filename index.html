3D Scan Gallery

Heritage objects and building elements documented with terrestrial laser scanning, processed to web-ready models. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. On a phone, tap View in your space to place the object at true physical size.

Capture: FARO Focus TLS · Registration: FARO SCENE · Meshing: CloudCompare (Poisson) · Retopology & texture baking: Blender · Delivery: glTF 2.0 (Draco-compressed). Models are to real-world scale.
